Overview概要

Born in Suzhou, China in 1989, he moved to Japan at age 10 with his father, a pianist. He began deep-learning research while still in university, and in 2015 was invited to lecture at Google Japan on autonomous driving technology using deep reinforcement learning. After graduating top of his class from Tokyo University of Technology, he joined DeNA's AI R&D division, where he conducted world-leading research in video-generation AI.

In 2019, with DeNA's backing, he founded DEVEL Inc. (now SYLA BRAIN), a startup that analyzes the real estate market with AI. After raising capital from both a SoftBank Group fund and a DeNA fund, the company formed a capital alliance with proptech firm SYLA in 2021, becoming a wholly owned subsidiary. As Executive Officer & CAIO (Chief AI Officer) of SYLA Technologies, he helped lead the company's NASDAQ listing in 2023 and SYLA HOLDINGS' TSE Standard Market listing in 2025. Having stepped down as an officer after the TSE listing, he now devotes himself to managing his family asset-management companies. An investor who develops and operates his own AI-based property price analysis systems, he puts his AI × real estate expertise to work in asset management.

Jun 2015

Invited Lecture at Google Japan — at the AI frontier since his student days

While still in university, he researched autonomous-driving simulation using deep reinforcement learning. The work earned him an invitation to speak at Google Japan. With an internship at Microsoft Japan and a win at the Nippon Venture Capital University Hackathon 2014, his technical talent stood out early — and he graduated top of his class from Tokyo University of Technology.

2016–2019

AI R&D at DeNA — presented world-leading video-generation AI research

At DeNA's Systems Division AI R&D group, he worked on computer vision research. At DeNA TechCon 2019 he presented research on frame interpolation — automatically generating the frames between video frames — achieving what was then world-leading performance, and drawing coverage from tech media such as ITmedia.

Oct 2019

Founded DEVEL Inc. — backed by SoftBank and DeNA funds

With DeNA's support he went independent, founding DEVEL Inc. (now SYLA BRAIN) to build systems that use AI to analyze and appraise all real estate data available online in Japan. Based at KERNEL HONGO — SoftBank Group's AI-focused incubation hub run by DEEPCORE — the company raised funding from a SoftBank Group fund and a DeNA fund in February 2020.

Jun 2020

Paper at JSAI2020 — then among Japan's most accurate real estate pricing AI

Built an AI model for high-accuracy real estate price estimation and presented it at JSAI2020, the annual conference of the Japanese Society for Artificial Intelligence. By incorporating Japan-specific price drivers such as condominium brands and location, the model far outperformed prior research and achieved what was then among the highest accuracy in Japan (8.35% mean error).

On March 31, 2023, SYLA Technologies listed on the NASDAQ in New York (ticker: SYT). Apr 2025

Rakumachi Channel — discussing AI × real estate with Takahiro Anno

In April 2025, he appeared on "The Future of AI × Real Estate," a talk program on the YouTube channel of the real estate investment platform Rakumachi, joining AI engineer, entrepreneur and science-fiction author Takahiro Anno and real estate consultant Osamu Nagashima (Chairman, Sakura Jimusho) to discuss how AI will transform real estate transactions.

On the program he presented "GOD AI," the real estate investment AI he built and has used for his own property purchases, explaining how it automatically collects the vast volume of listings posted to portals every day and uses AI to assess discounts and yields to surface promising deals. He also stressed that human judgment remains essential for factors the data cannot capture, such as a seller's circumstances or legal restrictions, and predicted that aesthetic value such as exterior design will increasingly be factored into AI's judgment.

Jun 2025

TSE Standard Market Listing — SYLA HOLDINGS (ticker: 8887)

Through a business combination with TSE Standard-listed CUMICA Co., Ltd. (ticker: 8887), CUMICA was renamed SYLA HOLDINGS — effectively completing the SYLA Group's listing on the Tokyo Stock Exchange.

He now devotes himself to managing his family asset-management companies, applying his AI × real estate expertise to property investment.
